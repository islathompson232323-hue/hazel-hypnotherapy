Questions

What to expect.

What is hypnosis?

A natural and deeply relaxing state of focused attention in which you remain fully aware, comfortable and in control throughout the session. It opens the subconscious mind to increased positive suggestions, helping you reframe negative thoughts and beliefs.

Have I experienced it in daily life?

Yes. Daydreaming, being absorbed in a book or film, driving somewhere and arriving without remembering the journey, or scrolling through social media are all everyday forms of it.

Can I be hypnotised?

Almost anyone can, and it’s a skill that improves with practice. Effectiveness relies on your commitment, belief and motivation, and is enhanced by a strong therapeutic relationship.

Is it dangerous?

No. You are always in control and cannot be made to do or say anything you don’t want to, and you cannot get stuck in hypnosis. The only time it may not be suitable is if you have a history of psychosis.

Why isn’t willpower enough?

Willpower lives in the conscious mind, which handles problem-solving and decisions. The subconscious is like a filing cabinet of memories, beliefs, learned behaviours and automatic responses, and its habits are often more powerful than the conscious mind.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the issue being addressed. After your initial consultation you’ll be given an idea of how many sessions may be required.

Do I need to tell my doctor?

Hypnotherapy can complement any medication or treatment you’re receiving. Keep following your doctor’s advice and taking prescribed medication, and let both your hypnotherapist and your doctor know about your treatments.
